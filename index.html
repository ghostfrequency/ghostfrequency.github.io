I work at the intersection of social science methodology, pedagogy, and public engagement.

As co-author of the world’s leading research methods textbooks and a Senior Fellow of the HEA, I specialize in communicating complex sociological ideas—whether designing innovative university curricula, training researchers, or translating archival studies of deviance and crime for international media broadcasts.

Core Pillars of Expertise

METHODOLOGY & PEDAGOGY

Social Science Methods

Expertise across qualitative, quantitative, and archival methodology, with a focus on active learning and digital curriculum design.

  • Textbook Author: Lead co-author of Bryman’s Social Research Methods (6th ed., OUP) and How To Do Your Social Research Project or Dissertation (OUP).
  • Educational Leadership: Director of Digital Education and Senate Award Fellow for sustained excellence in learning and teaching.
  • Curriculum Design: Developer of integrated 'methods spines' and interdisciplinary degree programs across 150+ modules.
RESEARCH & DEVIANCE

Sociology of Evil & Belief

Specialized research investigating deviance, high-profile criminal archives, supernatural belief systems, and social harm.

  • The Prison Files: Extensive archival analysis of notoriety, deviance management, and UK criminal history (Hindley, West, Shipman, Krays).
  • Sociology of Evil: Keynote speaker and author of an upcoming monograph on social frameworks of evil and morality.
  • Extraordinary Belief: Research on satanic panic narratives, folklore, and strategies of deviance management.
PUBLIC ENGAGEMENT

Media & Translation

Proven track record of communicating complex sociological concepts to national media, documentary teams, and broader public audiences.

  • Documentary & TV: On-screen expert and consultant for Trevor McDonald documentaries, ITN Productions, Wildfire TV, and BBC.
  • Press Commentary: 40+ media features across The Times, Daily Telegraph, Psychology Today, and Al Jazeera.
  • Public Engagement: Regular contributor to policy blogs (WONKHE, BERA) and public commentary platforms.
