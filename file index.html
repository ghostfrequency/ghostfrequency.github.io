About Me

I am an interdisciplinary sociologist and Lecturer in Research Methods at the University of Sheffield. My work spans the mechanics of social inquiry, the sociology of the uncanny, the cultural construction of "evil", and the pedagogy that brings research methods to life. Across all these areas, I focus on turning complex sociological research into accessible insights for students, academic communities, and the wider public.
