Research Pillar

The Sociology of Evil, Notoriety & Prison Archives

Investigating how society constructs, institutionalizes, and remembers extreme deviance. Through primary archival research in prison records, my work explores the social management of notoriety, moral panics, and the idea of evil.

Forthcoming Monograph: The Sociology of Evil

Moving beyond purely psychological or theological explanations, this project examines "evil" as a distinct sociological category. It explores how legal, penal, and media institutions define extraordinary harm, transform individuals into cultural symbols of absolute deviance, and manage the long-term societal fallout of high-profile crimes.

Primary Archival Research: The Prison Files

My empirical work relies heavily on historical prison documents, official correspondence, state records, and contemporary news archives to examine how infamous criminal figures were processed by both the state and the public imagination.
